# Journey Maps

Journey maps are discovery views over the customer experience. They link to Problems, Opportunities, Personas, and evidence; they do not replace those notes.

No approved live journey map exists yet. The case template is [JOURNEY-001 — imaging path](../../99-Playbook/Templates/JOURNEY-001-Imaging-Path.md).

## What belongs here

- current-state journey map
- future-state journey map, clearly labelled as a hypothesis
- service blueprint when backstage actors and systems matter
- links to the Problems and Opportunities the map supports

## What does not belong here

- a journey map with no evidence or source labels
- a duplicate Problem or Opportunity note
- a feature list disguised as a journey
