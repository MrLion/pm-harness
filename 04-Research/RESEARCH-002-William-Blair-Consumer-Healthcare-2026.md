# Research — William Blair Consumer-Centric Healthcare 2026

## Question this answers

Do U.S. healthcare consumers receive useful cost estimates before care, and what do they say about
acting on them?

## Method

Desk research on [William Blair, *Consumer-Centric Healthcare 2026*](https://www.williamblair.com/-/media/downloads/eqr/2026/williamblair_consumer-centric-healthcare-2026-update.pdf),
citing [Experian Health, *2025 State of Patient Access*](https://www.experian.com/content/dam/noindex/na/us/healthcare/state-of-patient-access-2025.pdf)
(survey of 213 healthcare employees and 1,004 patients); [Robert Wood Johnson Foundation data, reported by William Blair](https://www.williamblair.com/-/media/downloads/eqr/2026/williamblair_consumer-centric-healthcare-2026-update.pdf).

## Findings

- **Finding.** Estimate receipt is rising while perceived accuracy is falling.
  - Evidence: 41% received an estimate in 2025, up from 29% in 2022; 71% said it was accurate to
    $400, down from 78% in 2022.
  - Confidence: medium — large survey, but secondary reporting.
- **Finding.** Patients say they would postpone care without an accurate estimate.
  - Evidence: 43% said they would likely postpone care without an accurate estimate.
  - Confidence: low for behaviour — this is stated intent, not observed cancellation.
- **Finding.** Medical debt is widespread among patients who carry it.
  - Evidence: 73.6% of surveyed patients carried medical debt; 66.1% of those were in collections.
  - Confidence: medium for the surveyed population; not specific to imaging or HDHPs.

## Raw signal

> "43% indicate they will likely postpone care without an accurate estimate." — [William Blair](https://www.williamblair.com/-/media/downloads/eqr/2026/williamblair_consumer-centric-healthcare-2026-update.pdf), citing [Experian Health](https://www.experian.com/content/dam/noindex/na/us/healthcare/state-of-patient-access-2025.pdf)

## What this does NOT tell us

- Whether the 43% actually cancel or postpone.
- Whether respondents are employer-sponsored HDHP members choosing imaging. Do not transfer the 41%, 71%, or 43% figures to that segment without segment-level evidence.
- Whether an estimate would change a booking decision.
- Whether estimates are accurate enough for a specific service.
