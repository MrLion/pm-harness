> [!example]- Worked example — fictional
> **Question:** how do clinics actually cover an unexpected gap today?
> **Method:** 11 semi-structured interviews with practice managers, Jan–Feb, 20–40 min.
> **Finding:** 9 of 11 run a serial phone tree; median 6 calls per gap; 4 keep a hand-written
> reliability ranking. Confidence: medium (self-report, small N).
> **Quote:** "I know exactly who'll say yes, it's just that I have to ring all of them in order."
