> [!example]- Worked example — fictional
> **Priority thesis:** If we auto-offer shift gaps to eligible staff instead of requiring the
> manager to choose, then median time-to-fill drops below 15 minutes, because 9 of 11 interviewed
> managers describe the delay as decision latency, not staff shortage.
>
> **Differentiation**
> | Alternative | Does well | Fails our group | Our answer |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Deputy | Full workforce suite | Onboarding takes weeks for a 12-person clinic | Two-field setup, live in 10 minutes |
> | Spreadsheet + group chat | Free, familiar | No audit trail, no rest-period check | Keep the familiar feel, add the guardrail |
>
> **Not doing:** payroll, time-and-attendance, HR records. **Invalidated if** clinics say the
> delay is staffing shortage rather than decision latency.
