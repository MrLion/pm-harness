# Product Vision Board

Replaces `00-Product/Product.md`. Five sections. The vision is enduring; the four below it are
strategy elements and are **assumptions to be validated**, not facts to be declared.

> What is the reason for creating the product? What positive change should it create?

> Inspiring · Shared · Ethical · Concise · Ambitious · Enduring (guides the next 5–10 years)

> [!example]- Worked example — fictional product, delete this block before use
> **Product: Lantern** — shift scheduling for small outpatient clinics.
>
> **Vision:** No clinic manager spends a Sunday building next week's rota.
> *Inspiring ✓ Shared ✓ Ethical ✓ Concise ✓ Ambitious ✓ Enduring ✓*
>
> **Target group:** Practice managers in 5–20-staff outpatient clinics, 1–3 locations, currently
> scheduling by spreadsheet and group chat. Clear ✓ Specific (5–20 staff; below 5 they just ring
> each other) ✓ Cohesive ✓
>
> **Needs** (prioritised):
> | # | Need | Evidence | Confidence |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | Fill an unexpected shift gap without phoning six people | 11 interviews, 9 describe the same phone tree | medium |
> | 2 | Avoid breaching rest-period rules when covering gaps | 4 interviews, 1 near-miss audit finding | low |
>
> **Product** — stand-out features:
> | # | Feature | Differentiator |
> |---|---|---|
> | 1 | Gap auto-offer to eligible staff in rank order | Competitors require the manager to pick manually |
> | 2 | Mobile accept/decline in two taps | Rivals are desktop-first |
> | 3 | Rest-period guardrail that blocks an illegal assignment | Rivals warn but allow |
>
> **Business goals:** 200 paying clinics in 18 months · monthly churn under 4% · CAC payback under 9 months.
>
> **Extended board:** Competitors — Deputy, Planday, spreadsheets. Revenue — £29/clinic/month.
> Costs — support-heavy onboarding. Channels — clinic-manager communities, accountant referrals.
