# Decision — build, partner, or defer?

## Question

Should we build, partner, or defer on a transparent outpatient estimate?

## Decision

Proposed: defer build. Run the journey research and the data-access review first.

## Why

- The evidence is market-level, not segment-level.
- The buyer is unresolved.
- Estimate data access and reconciliation are unresolved.

## Options considered

| Option | What it buys | What it gives up |
|---|---|---|
| Build | Control of the estimate experience | Speed, cost, reversibility |
| Partner | Faster data access | Control and partner dependency |
| Defer | Learning first | Near-term patient impact |
| Status quo | No new spend | The current cost surprise continues |

## Reversal trigger

A documented segment finding plus evidence that estimate data can be accessed and reconciled.
