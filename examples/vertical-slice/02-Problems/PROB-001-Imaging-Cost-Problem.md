# Problem — out-of-pocket cost uncertainty before outpatient imaging

## Problem statement

Adults on employer-sponsored high-deductible plans cannot reliably predict their final patient responsibility before booking elective outpatient imaging. Some receive an estimate; coverage and accuracy are uneven.

## Evidence

The survey evidence is broader than this segment:

- 41% received an estimate.
- 71% of those said it was accurate to $400.
- 43% said they would likely postpone without an accurate estimate — stated intent, not observed behaviour.

Source: [RESEARCH-002](../../../04-Research/RESEARCH-002-William-Blair-Consumer-Healthcare-2026.md).

## Segment note

Frequency and accuracy for employer-sponsored HDHP members choosing imaging are unknown. Do not transfer the survey percentages to this segment.
