# Product — transparent outpatient imaging

## Vision

Adults on employer-sponsored HDHPs can predict what care will cost before they commit.

## Target group

Adults on employer-sponsored HDHPs choosing elective outpatient imaging.

## Need

Know the expected patient responsibility before booking, and understand what the estimate includes and excludes.

## Product

A patient-readable, pre-booking estimate for one imaging type, with a reconciliation check against the final bill.

## Buyer

Unresolved: employer, payer, or provider.

## Non-goals

Provider matching, scheduling, claims adjudication, and multi-service estimates in the first pilot.
