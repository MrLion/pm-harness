# Digital Transformation

Use when the unit of change is a **portfolio or enterprise capability**, not a single product. The
work is to change how the organization delivers digital outcomes: capabilities, value streams,
product boundaries, funding, governance, and adoption measurement.

This is not a product discovery loop. Do not run a single-product Problem → Opportunity workflow and
call it transformation.

## What good looks like

- The transformation thesis names the capability change, not a technology purchase.
- Product boundaries are explicit enough to assign ownership and funding.
- Each product has a vision, strategy, goals, and a North Star.
- Adoption is measured by changed work, not by training completion.
