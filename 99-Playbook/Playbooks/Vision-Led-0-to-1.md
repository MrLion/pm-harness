# Vision-Led 0→1

Use when the product or market does not exist yet, or when the team is entering a new segment with
a new value proposition. The entry artifact is a **Product Vision Board**, not a feature request.

## Gates

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Vision clarity | Target group, need, and future state are explicit and falsifiable |
| Evidence discipline | Facts, assumptions, and hypotheses are labeled separately |
| Riskiest assumption | The team can name the one assumption that would kill the thesis |
| Concept evidence | At least one cheap test has run before build commitment |
| MVP scope | The MVP tests the thesis, not a feature list |
| Review | The outcome is compared with the original vision, not just delivery output |

## What good looks like

- The vision is written before the problem backlog.
- Strategy explains why now and why us, not just what to build.
- Goals and the North Star measure customer value, not activity.
- The Opportunity Solution Tree starts from the desired outcome, not from a feature request.
- A PRD is produced only after the riskiest assumptions have evidence.

## Do not start with

- A feature request or a competitor teardown
- A PRD with no validated problem
- A roadmap with dates before the thesis is testable
