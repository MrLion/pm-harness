# Opportunity — compare providers on cost and network fit

## The ten questions

**1. Exactly what problem will this solve?**
Consumers struggle to compare providers on price, network fit, and review quality before choosing care.

**2. For whom do we solve that problem?**
Adults on employer-sponsored HDHPs choosing outpatient imaging.

**3. How big is the opportunity?**
Unknown. Provider comparison depends on estimate coverage and accuracy first.

**4. What alternatives are out there?**
Payer directories, provider websites, third-party review sites, and informal referrals.

**5. Why are we best suited to pursue this?**
Unknown. The differentiator would be combining cost clarity with network fit and availability.

**6. Why now?**
Not now. The estimate pilot must establish usable cost data before provider comparison is credible.

**7. How will we get this to market?**
Unknown. Provider or payer data access is unresolved.

**8. How will we measure success / make money?**
Unknown. Candidate measure: share of provider comparisons that include cost, network, and availability.

**9. What factors are critical to success?**
Provider data access, accurate network status, comparable quality signals, and a clear buyer.

**10. Given the above, what's the recommendation?**
Defer until the estimate pilot proves coverage and accuracy. Provider comparison would otherwise repeat the original data-access risk.

## Four big risks

| Risk | Rating | Load-bearing assumption | How we'll test it | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Value | unknown | Patients want provider comparison, not only a cost estimate | Recent-decision interviews | PM | untested |
| Usability | unknown | Patients can interpret network and cost differences | Prototype test | Design | untested |
| Feasibility | unknown | Provider data is complete and current | Data-access review | Eng | untested |
| Business viability | unknown | A buyer values provider comparison | Buyer interviews | PM | untested |

## Recommendation

Defer. Revisit after the estimate pilot establishes coverage and reconciled accuracy.
