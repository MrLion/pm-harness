# JTBD — choose imaging with cost clarity

## Job story

When I need elective outpatient imaging and my deductible is not met, I want to know what I will actually owe before I book, so I can decide whether to proceed, compare in-network providers, or delay.

## Current alternatives

- Call the provider or insurer.
- Use a portal estimate.
- Ask for a cash price.
- Compare online or ask friends.

## Segment conditions

| Condition | Why it changes the job |
|---|---|
| Deductible met or not met | Determines whether the patient is paying full price or a co-pay |
| Elective or urgent | Urgent care reduces the room to compare or delay |
| In-network or out-of-network | Changes the data and the financial exposure |
| One procedure or several | Changes the estimate scope and error |

## Desired outcomes

| Outcome | Importance | Satisfaction today | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Know expected out-of-pocket cost | high | unknown | low |
| Understand what is included and excluded | high | unknown | low |
| Compare providers on cost | medium | unknown | low |
