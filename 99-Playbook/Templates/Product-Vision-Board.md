# Product Vision Board — transparent outpatient imaging

## Vision

Adults on employer-sponsored HDHPs can choose care before care chooses them.

## Target Group

Adults on employer-sponsored HDHPs choosing outpatient imaging.

## Needs

| # | Need | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Know expected out-of-pocket cost before booking | [RESEARCH-002](../../04-Research/RESEARCH-002-William-Blair-Consumer-Healthcare-2026.md) | low |
| 2 | Understand what the estimate includes and excludes | [RESEARCH-002](../../04-Research/RESEARCH-002-William-Blair-Consumer-Healthcare-2026.md) | low |

## Product

| # | Stand-out feature | Differentiator vs alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Patient-readable estimate with inclusions, exclusions, and validity | Hypothesis: existing estimators are hard to interpret |
| 2 | Reconciliation against final patient responsibility | Hypothesis: accuracy is the trust barrier |

## Business Goals

| # | Business goal | Rough target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Increase pre-booking estimate coverage | 60% of eligible requests in pilot |
| 2 | Improve reconciled accuracy | 70% within $400 in pilot |

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Competitors | Payer estimators, provider-portal estimators, third-party price comparison, human-assisted estimates |
| Revenue sources | Unknown — buyer unresolved |
| Cost factors | Data access, reconciliation, support |
| Channels | Provider, payer, employer, direct patient |
